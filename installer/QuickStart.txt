CHORDBLOOM - QUICK START
========================

Chordbloom is installed. In FL Studio:

1. Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (or "Start scan").
2. Channel Rack "+" > Chordbloom (pick the VST3 version).
3. Click a chord pad - you'll hear Chordbloom's built-in sound.

BUILD A PROGRESSION
- Double-click pads (or drag them) into the Progression lane, then press Play.
- No idea where to start? Click "Spark" for a fresh progression, or "Presets".
- Glowing pads = chords that sound good next. Right-click a chord in the lane
  for more options (change chord, length, inversion, delete...).

PLAY IT ON YOUR OWN SYNTH (e.g. Omnisphere)
- Chordbloom wrapper: gear icon > MIDI > Output port = 1
- Your synth's wrapper: MIDI > Input port = 1
- Click the speaker icon in Chordbloom's top bar to mute its built-in sound,
  otherwise you hear both.

GET THE MIDI INTO YOUR PROJECT
- Drag the "Chords", "Bass" or "Both" chips (bottom right) onto the playlist
  or piano roll - or drag a single pad / chord straight out of the plugin.
- Then switch off "Play with FL" so the chords don't play twice.

The "?" button in the top-right corner of the plugin shows all of this again.

Also installed: a Standalone Chordbloom app (Start menu) for playing around
without FL.
